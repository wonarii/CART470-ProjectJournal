### First Client Meeting

We got to meet Elizabeth Miller! I'm very glad she's passionate about this project, it makes the work more enjoyable. Her feedback is relevant and it seems she has a good vision of what she's expecting of this project.

![The team with Liz](/assets/FirstClientMeeting.jpg)

I was surprised when I heard her mention a museum exposition, I had not thought about using the interactive map in that kind of context. Still, it's an interesting challenge to think about since it changes the conditions a user would be using this map in.

Speaking to Lizreally helped better understand the project but also learn more about her vision and preferred aesthetics (more mature looking, artistic, poetic, minimalism... Not my usual cartoon looks and bright colours). She also placed emphasis on some elements more than others for the focus of the map (like pollution and connection).

At the end of the meeting, we also all established goals for this project. I had no idea what kind of goal she meant so I tunnel visioned on the seagulls because that's my favourite part of the project.

Our goals are:
1. Scalable (Liz should be able to add in more information and connections to the map easily without having to go edit code)
2. Seagulls (this one is mine) Connections and paths between more than one species, showing the islands through another lense than just human.
3. Learning more about the archipelago (we want to learn while creating this project)
4. Usable and functional (Tianshun just wants the map to work lollll)
5. Good user experience for the audience despite it being unfamiliar to us (museum goers)
